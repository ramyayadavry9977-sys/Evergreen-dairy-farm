GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions.

Find answers to common questions about our dairy products, delivery services, and farm.

What dairy products do you offer?

We offer a range of dairy products, including fresh milk and other dairy essentials. Contact our team to learn about current product availability.

Do you provide home delivery?

Delivery availability depends on your location. Contact us with your area details to confirm whether home delivery is available.

Can I place bulk orders for my business?

Yes, you can contact us to discuss bulk order quantities, product availability, pricing, and delivery arrangements.

Can I visit your dairy farm?

Please contact us in advance to ask about farm visits, available timings, and any arrangements needed before your visit.

How can I enquire about your prices?

Use the contact form above to tell us which products you are interested in. Our team can provide the relevant pricing information.
