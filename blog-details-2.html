Keep It Fresh: Simple Tips for Storing Dairy Products

Small storage habits can make a big difference. Discover practical ways to handle milk, curd, paneer, butter, and other dairy essentials in your everyday kitchen.

Written by Evergreen Editorial Team
Dairy Guide
4 min read
F

Freshness begins with the way we handle food at home. Dairy products are part of many everyday meals, but each product has its own storage needs. Following the label instructions, keeping refrigerators at a suitable temperature, and using clean utensils can help maintain quality and reduce the risk of spoilage.

01. Store Milk Properly

Return milk to the refrigerator promptly after serving. Keep it in its original container whenever possible, close the lid securely, and follow the storage directions printed on the package.

Avoid leaving milk at room temperature for extended periods. When serving a meal, pour the amount you need into a clean glass or jug instead of leaving the main container on the table.

02. Keep Curd Covered and Chilled

Store curd in a clean, covered container and keep it refrigerated according to the product label. Always use a clean, dry spoon when serving it.

Avoid returning leftover curd from a serving bowl to the original container. This simple habit helps reduce the chance of introducing unwanted contamination.

03. Handle Paneer with Care

Paneer is a fresh dairy product that needs appropriate refrigeration. Keep it covered in its original packaging until needed, and follow the manufacturer's instructions after opening.

If the packaging provides specific storage instructions, follow those directions rather than relying on a general storage method. Use paneer within the recommended period and discard it if there are signs of spoilage.

04. Protect Butter and Cheese

Keep butter and cheese covered and refrigerated according to their packaging. Securely wrapping them helps protect against drying, unwanted odours, and contact with other foods.

Use clean utensils when handling these products. For cheese varieties with different storage needs, check the package for guidance on temperature and how long the product can be kept after opening.

05. Make Your Refrigerator Work Better

Keep your refrigerator at or below 4°C (40°F), and use a refrigerator thermometer if necessary to check the temperature. Store dairy products in appropriate refrigerated areas, and avoid overcrowding the refrigerator so cool air can circulate.

Check expiry dates and follow any use-by instructions. Arrange products so older items that are still within their recommended storage period are easy to find, and clean shelves and spills regularly.

Your Freshness Checklist

Refrigerate dairy promptly, close packages securely, use clean utensils, check labels, and keep your refrigerator at or below 4°C (40°F). When in doubt about a product's safety, discard it.
